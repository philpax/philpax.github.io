[lunette](https://github.com/philpax/lunette) is an experimental, scriptable XR workspace for Linux. It can launch Wayland applications and mirror monitors in a headset, either as an overlay or as a standalone application. Its longer-term goal is a complete workspace that can be used without a separate desktop environment.
