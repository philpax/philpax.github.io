[exoform](https://github.com/philpax/exoform) is an experimental collaborative modelling tool built around signed distance fields, which represent shapes through their distance from a surface. It combines a Bevy and egui application with Saft's geometry-processing tools.

<!-- more -->

## opensaft

[opensaft](https://github.com/philpax/opensaft) is the related fork of Embark Studios' Saft library for compiling, interpreting, discretising, and meshing signed distance fields. The fork makes the source more accessible and replaces `macaw` with `glam`.
