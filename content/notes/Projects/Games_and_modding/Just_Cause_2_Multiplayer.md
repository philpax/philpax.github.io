[Just Cause 2: Multiplayer](https://store.steampowered.com/app/259080/Just_Cause_2_Multiplayer_Mod/) is a multiplayer mod for Avalanche Studios' Just Cause 2. I co-led development alongside the project's founder, and joined early in its development.

<!-- more -->

As part of my work, I led scripting, general product management, and public-facing writing. I also developed several internal tools, including a shader decompiler and a network packet definition compiler.
