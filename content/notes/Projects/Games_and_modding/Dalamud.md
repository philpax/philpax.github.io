[Dalamud](https://github.com/goatcorp/Dalamud) is a plugin framework for Final Fantasy 14. Through a series of bizarre events that I cannot relate to you, I ended up as the architect and head of its Plugin Approval Committee, as well as the designer of its [plugin submission process](https://github.com/goatcorp/DIPs/blob/main/text/17-automated-build-and-submit-pipeline.md). Hundreds of plugins have been merged under my aegis. For the record, I haven't played past the base game, and unsubscribed in 2023.
