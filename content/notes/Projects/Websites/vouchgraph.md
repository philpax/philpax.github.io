[vouchgraph](https://github.com/philpax/vouchgraph) visualises the relationships recorded by [atvouch](https://atvouch.dev/)[^atvouch] as a graph.

[^atvouch]: An experimental atproto-based vouch network by my friend [lun-4](https://l4.pm/) that I had some design input on.

<!-- more -->

The graph is semi-live: incoming updates are collected, but are not applied to the displayed graph automatically, because online graph updates are apparently extraordinarily difficult for visualisation libraries to manage.
