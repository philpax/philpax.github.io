[ida-c-splitter](https://github.com/ferrobrew/ida-c-splitter) turns a monolithic C or C++ export from IDA's Hex-Rays decompiler into a navigable source tree. It splits functions and type definitions into source and header files organised around namespaces and classes, making the output easier to search and inspect.
