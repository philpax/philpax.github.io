[paxsite](https://github.com/philpax/philpax.github.io) is the custom Rust static-site generator behind this website. It renders blog posts, updates, and hierarchical notes from Markdown, with shared tags and links between them. It uses [paxhtml](paxhtml.md) for HTML templating and generates the site's styles, subset fonts, and OpenGraph images during the build.
