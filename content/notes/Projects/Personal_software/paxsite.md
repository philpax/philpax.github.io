[paxsite](https://github.com/philpax/philpax.github.io) is the custom Rust static site generator that runs this place. SSGs are fun to write, and I'm afraid of getting rug-pulled by my choice of ecosystem (as has happened to me in the past), so I went with something that I knew I could trust and maintain going into the future. It uses [paxhtml](paxhtml.md) for HTML templating.
