[blackbird](https://github.com/philpax/blackbird) is my Rust music player, targeting Subsonic-compatible servers (specifically, Navidrome). It's designed around a dense terminal interface, reminiscent of my old foobar2000 setup. I created it because I wanted an efficient cross-platform player that behaves how _I_ want it to behave.
